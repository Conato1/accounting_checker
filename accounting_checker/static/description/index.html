The Unbalanced Books Risk

Corrupted data migrations, third-party sync failures, or manual overrides often leave behind unbalanced journal entries (Debit ≠ Credit), orphan ghost invoices, and sequence gaps that trigger severe tax penalties and failed external financial audits.

The XodetiStudio Solution

An automated one-click integrity inspector that evaluates thousands of ledger rows. Provides an instant Database Health Score, identifies the exact user and timestamp for every faulty transaction, and delivers automated daily email alerts.
